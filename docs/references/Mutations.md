    (23.33%) - Springy Tendons: +10% jump height

    (3.35%) - Gigantism: Increases species size by 2%

    (0.84%) - Double Jump: Lets you jump one more time mid-air!

# Special Mutations (5% per roll)
    - Alien: Coat top, coat bottom, hair, sock and pattern are darkened and retextured with black branching tendrils and glowing spots. The species' eyes glow white and a pattern colored whispy halo swirls around the species' neck. 
    - Arcane: Covers your horse in mystical arcane sigils.
    - Arisen: Coat Top and Coat Bottom are re-textured with a blue tinted crystal-like pattern, Hair and Hoof becomes transparent, Sock and Keratin becomes glowing. Certain colors can overpower the blue texture. The mount's eyes also glow white and a pattern-colored halo sits around the neck. 
    - Artisan: Covers your horse in a bright artisan design.
    - Astral: Causes your horse to gain a veil of cosmic energy.
    - Crystallized: Entire species is retextured with a Cyan crystal texture. Certain colors can overpower the cyan.
    - Dilute: Transforms all colors to a much whiter version, giving your horse a pastel appearance.
    - Festive Eye: The species' eyes shift between glowing green and red. 
    - Flowering Sakura: Coat Bottom, Hair, and Sock are retextured with sakura petals. The species' eyes glow white and Sock colored petals fall from their legs. 
    - Frost: Recolors the species' base colors to an icy blue with a white snowflake pattern. Certain colors can overpower the blue. A snowflake halo swirls around the species' neck, and the eyes glow light blue. 
    - Geode: Transforms your horse into a geode with crystal regions!
    - Ghost: Makes the species' base features see-through. 
    - Ghoul: Makes the species' secondary features (Hair, Paint, Pattern, Keratin colored regions) see-through. 
    - Gilded: Adds a gold foil texture overriding the colors of the hoof, keratin, paint, nose, and sock. The color region influences the mutation color. 
    - Glow: A single part of the horse will glow with a magical aura!(Can only apply to Hoof, Hair, Paint, Keratin, Pattern, Sock, or Eyes regions)
    - Haunting: All regions except Pattern and Paint become see-through, the eyes are solid black, and mini white spheres circle the mount's head. 
    - Jester: Adds a Checkered Texture overriding the colors of the socks, mane and pattern, alongside black diamond leg particles.
    - Lacebound: Causes your horse to appear adorned in gothic lace. (Applies to Coat Top, Coat Bottom and Sock. The legs have heart particles.)
    - North Star: Encases your horse in stars.
    - Porcelain: Hoof, Paint, Pattern, and Keratin are retextured with porcelain pattern. The species' eyes glow dark blue along with glowing Sock. 
    - Reptile: Adds a scale pattern texture overiding the colors of the socks, mane, and pattern. 
    - Rose Infection: Infects your horse with a rose-like texture and glowing eyes. (Applies to Paint, Pattern, Keratin, and Eyes)
    - Tie Dye: Adds a Tie Dye texture overriding the colors of the hair, hoof, sock, coat top and coat bottom. The color region influences the mutation color. 
    - Trophy: Entire species apart from eyes is retextured with a smooth, metallic material. Color is dictated by the tier of the trophy mutation. 
    - X Ray: Makes every feature see-through and the skeleton becomes visible. 

# Mutated (these are the special spawns but they get one of these mutations by default)
    Solar: Entire species is re-painted in red and yellows hues with a bright flare texture and a magical solar neck ring. 
    
    Lunar: Entire species is re-painted in blue hues with a sparkling moon texture and a magical lunar neck ring. 
    
    Heavenly: Species' coat becomes a pure white with glowing parts, a neck halo, and black glow eyes. 
    
    Void: Species' coat becomes a pure black with glowing parts, a neck halo, and white glow eyes. 
    
    Astral: Species' coat becomes textured with a star pattern, along with purple glowing parts.
