# Personalities
## Anxious
The mount emits the startled emote when being dismounted, player is idle, near a crowd or loud event. If the mount is near other mounts or the player walks away, the sweat drop emote is emitted.

## Affectionate
The mount emits the sad emote when the player walks away from them. When the player is idle, or the mount is fed, the nuzzle emote is emitted. If mounted or near other mounts, the heart effect appears.

## Clingy
It emits the nuzzle emote when mounted, and the sad emote when dismounted or when the player walks away from it. Mount emits nuzzle emote when near other mounts. When the mount is near a crowd or a loud event, they follow the player more closely.

## Curious
The mount emits the question mark emote if a Clue is nearby, when player is idle, mounted, near other mounts or crowd and when hearing a loud event.

## Energetic
When the player is idle, 8 point star effect is emitted. When mounted, rays effect or excited emote is emitted. Being fed results in sparkles effect being emitted. If the player walks away, then the mount follows them quickly. Dismounted results in blep emote being emitted.

## Grumpy
Mounts always emit both hoof stomp and angry puff emotes in quick succession just after being equipped. The mount emits the angry puff emote when being mounted, and the hoof stomp emote when being dismounted. When the mount is groomed, player is idle, near a crowd or loud event, the angry puff emote is emitted.

## Lazy
Usually emits the tired emote when being groomed or if the player is idle. Mounts frequently tends to lie down. When the player walks away, mounts slowly follow them.

## Playful
Tends to walk around in circles a lot, and rarely lies down. Approaches other equipped mounts & the player with a tail wag emote. When mounted and dismounted, the blep emote is emitted. When the player walks away, mounts follow playfully.
